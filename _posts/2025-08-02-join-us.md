🔥 Are you passionate about research in communication networks, cloud computing, AI technologies, and cybersecurity?

## HIGHLIGHTED RESEARCH DIRECTIONS

💡 **Distributed AI**: Researching large-scale AI infrastructure deployment and security in edge-cloud computing environments.  
💡 **Cloud Network Security**: Researching and implementing methods to detect and mitigate DDoS attacks in cloud computing environments.  
💡 **Edge-Cloud Computing**: Researching and implementing optimization algorithms and reinforcement learning to improve the performance of distributed serverless systems.  
💡 **5G/6G Network Slicing**: Researching and developing platforms integrating optimization and reinforcement learning algorithms to optimize resource management in 5G/6G network slicing systems.

🔑 Access to modern infrastructure for network research purposes.  
🔑 Participation in training courses and seminars on cutting-edge science, technology, and engineering topics hosted by the research group.  
🔑 Opportunities to co-author scientific papers and publish in prestigious conferences and journals.  
🔑 Dedicated support and mentorship from faculty and lab members.  
🔑 Participation in large projects in collaboration with domestic and international companies and organizations.  
🔑 Opportunities for studying abroad or working overseas after graduation.  
🔑 Participation in recreational activities and team-building events designed to foster collaboration and research skills.
